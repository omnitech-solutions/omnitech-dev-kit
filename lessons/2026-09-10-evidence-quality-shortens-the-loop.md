# Evidence quality, not model size, shortens the loop
Observed: with a precise root cause in the reader's evidence note (one DOM default action), a fast model produced a 14-line fix, a teethed test and a receipt in 3.5 minutes for $0.015. The same model with a guessed design needed two fix loops and $0.17.
Caught by: the spend ledger, compared unit by unit.
Rule: spend the human minutes on the evidence note and the packet, not on a bigger implementer model. Reader questions name at most five files; orchestrator prompts name exact line ranges or none.
Evidence: legion-os-surveys FF-04 vs FF-01 in spend.md.
